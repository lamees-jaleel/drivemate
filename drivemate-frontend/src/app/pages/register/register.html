Information presented by DriveMate is intended to support vehicle management and decision making. It should not replace inspection or repair by a qualified automotive professional when professional assistance is required.

Emergency and roadside assistance features depend on available information, location services and participating responders. Users should contact appropriate emergency services whenever immediate danger exists.

Users must not misuse DriveMate, attempt unauthorized access, upload malicious files, impersonate another person, or use the platform for fraudulent or unlawful purposes.

9. Changes to These Terms

These terms may be updated as DriveMate functionality changes. Updated terms should be reviewed before continued use of the platform.

DRIVEMATE PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes the types of information DriveMate may process and how that information is used within the platform.

1. Personal Information

DriveMate may process information such as your name, phone number, email address, address and selected account role.

2. Vehicle Information

Vehicle information may include registration details, ownership records, maintenance history, expenses, reminders and other vehicle lifecycle records entered by users.

3. Professional Information

Professional users may provide qualifications, specialization, experience, organization information, certificate information, service location and verification documents for administrator review.

4. Uploaded Documents

Documents uploaded to DriveMate may contain vehicle, insurance, compliance or professional verification information. Access should be restricted to authorized platform functions and authorized users.

5. Location Information

Location information may be processed when location-based or roadside assistance functionality is used and where the user permits location access.

6. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to manage accounts, maintain vehicle lifecycle records, generate reminders, support verification workflows, provide relevant platform functionality and improve system security.

7. Security

DriveMate should apply appropriate technical and organizational controls to protect stored personal, vehicle and professional information against unauthorized access or modification.

8. Information Sharing

Information should only be shared where needed to provide requested services, support approved professional interactions, comply with legal requirements, or protect platform security.

9. Data Retention

Information should be retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, subject to applicable operational and legal requirements.

10. User Choices

Users should be able to review and update appropriate account information and may request account-related changes in accordance with DriveMate's available account management functionality.
